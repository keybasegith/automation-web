WEEKLY REPORT - KEYBASE FINANCIAL GROUP WEBSITE

Week of:     June 22-26, 2026
Prepared by: Dax Sukhraj
Project:     Keybase Financial Group corporate website (Next.js)
Status:      Built, committed, and deployed to production (Vercel)

====================================================================

SUMMARY

This week I built out and launched the Keybase Financial Group website.
The work covered the full public-facing site - homepage, About,
leadership pages, an advisor directory, a new Newsroom, and a new
Careers section with a working job-application form - plus site-wide
navigation (a services mega-menu and a language selector). All changes
were committed to GitHub and deployed live on Vercel.

====================================================================

WHAT WAS BUILT AND CHANGED

1. HOMEPAGE
   - Renamed the four service categories under "A complete approach to
     your financial life" to Wealth Planning, Financial Advice,
     Investment Solutions, and Preservation Strategies, and rewrote the
     supporting copy for each while keeping the existing design.
   - Reworked the hero video messaging: the second slide now reads
     "Powered by AI, guided by people," positioning Keybase as an early
     adopter of AI paired with human judgment.
   - Updated the featured carousel to use a cleaner advisor-consultation
     image on the lead slide.

2. ABOUT PAGE (FULL REBUILD)
   - Replaced the old content with new, structured sections:
     - Hero with a looping video background and "Independent Advice.
       National Reach. Built for the Future."
     - Who We Are and Our Vision narrative sections.
     - What Defines Us - six redesigned, elevated value cards
       (Independent by Design, Human-Centered, Built on Trust,
       Entrepreneurial at the Core, National in Reach, Committed to
       Impact) with hover effects and numbered accents.
     - The Keybase Standard section with a full-width background image
       and tuned overlay.
   - Removed the outdated stats band (which contained placeholder
     figures) and the bottom call-to-action block.

3. CEO MESSAGE PAGE
   - Removed the dark banner and restyled the top to match the
     leadership pages (breadcrumb + "Message from the CEO" heading).
   - Moved the CEO portrait to the right and center-aligned the
     name/title.
   - Published the new CEO letter (founder & president message) with
     consistent typography.

4. KEY EXECUTIVES PAGE
   - Built an interactive leadership grid where clicking a person
     expands their bio directly beneath their row.
   - Final lineup across three rows, with "Coming Soon" placeholders for
     photos still to be supplied:
     - Dax Sukhraj (President & CEO)
     - Don Cook (VP, Chief Financial Officer)
     - Keith Sutherland (VP, System Development and Support)
     - Krissy Sukhraj (Director of Marketing & Corporate Strategy)
     - Mark Garcia (Chief Compliance Officer) - new profile photo
     - Pushpa Shivanthan (VP, Back Office Administration)
     - Jerome Pare (Senior I.T. Specialist)
   - Cleaned up the layout: compact bios, square/sharp portrait frames
     with adjusted height, "Read a message from our CEO" shown only for
     the CEO, and removed the "View full profile" buttons.

5. OUR ADVISORS PAGE (REDESIGN)
   - Replaced the old hero with a clean breadcrumb + heading matching the
     leadership pages.
   - Rebuilt the advisor cards into a professional directory format:
     photo, name & role, email and phone, divider, and a concise list of
     specialties.
   - Populated real contact details for the current advisors (Darko
     Strukan, Neil Alford, Shomari Hutchinson, Johnathan Leung) and added
     "Coming Soon" placeholders where photos are pending.
   - Added a second and third row of additional advisor placeholders to
     show the full team structure.

6. NEWSROOM PAGE (NEW)
   - Created a dedicated Newsroom with a category filter bar (Markets,
     Retirement, Investing, Tax & Estate, Insurance, Firm News), a live
     search, and an article list - all styled to the Keybase brand.

7. CAREERS PAGE (NEW) WITH ONLINE APPLICATIONS
   - Built a recruiting page with a hero, a list of open roles (Financial
     Advisor, Associate Advisor, Compliance Analyst, Client Service
     Associate, Marketing Coordinator, IT Support Specialist), and a
     job-application form.
   - Clicking Apply on a role pre-fills the form and scrolls to it; there
     is also a "General Application" option.
   - Applications submit to a backend endpoint that validates the data,
     logs every submission, and can forward applications to email/HR
     systems once a webhook is configured.

8. SITE-WIDE NAVIGATION
   - Added an "Our Services" mega-menu with three columns: Wealth
     Planning, Investment Solutions (Plans + Products), and Preservation
     Strategies.
   - Added a language selector in the header (English, French, Chinese,
     Korean, Spanish, Hindi).
   - Wired the Newsroom and Careers menu links to the new pages.
   - Restyled the Contact Us button (filled by default, inverts on
     hover).
